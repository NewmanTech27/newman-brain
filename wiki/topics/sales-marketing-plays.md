## Content

### Competitive landscape
- 4 MX C&I rivals profiled: **Enlight** (HubSpot CMS, keyword-stuffed SEO), **Energía Real** (WAF-blocked, PPA Solar+BESS+MEM, ~1.80 MXN/kWh press estimate, HQ Reforma 296 CDMX), **Niko** (residential→commercial pivot, Webflow), **Quartux** (BESS-as-a-service, headcount surged ~10x to ~136). C&I assets are behind-the-meter GD, exempt from CRE generation permits; GD threshold moving 0.5→0.7 MW. Published report at lopezpalacios.github.io/newman-competitive-report.
- Regulatory read: Sheinbaum 2024–26 reform re-centers CFE (≥54% of energy injected) but courts private capital (Esquemas de Desarrollo Mixto) — not an AMLO-style blockade.

### SEO / TOFU whitespace
- Newman's whitespace = **live CFE tariff data + instant savings calculator + honest PPA math in MXN + CFE-compliance/post-2025-reform/glossary content** that competitors ignore.
- 568-keyword universe from Google Autocomplete (hl=es&gl=mx); 8 execution-ready briefs (e.g. "tarifa GDMTH explicada"). Pillar play: "Reforma 2025 y tu tarifa GDMTH" — dated, sourced volatility analysis.
- Funnel: ungated volatility chart teaser → savings calculator (with personalized volatility chart) → gated lead form (tariff + consumption + intent + **LFPDPPP consent**) → qualified leads only to salesman. Instrument conversion rates, not traffic. Blocked on real GDMTH pre/post-reform tariff numbers.
- Eval caveat: "Alchemy/Sutherland reframing" must be *applied* (one concrete psychological device per asset), not name-dropped; verify the "post-reform whitespace" claim with SERP/keyword volume before building.

### Assets
- **Energy Academy**: 7-track / 28-lesson EU energy sales course (PPA/solar/BESS/exit-to-funds/EU-funding), Czechia worked example, 4 JS calculators; live at newmantech27.github.io/newman-energy-academy.
- **Newman Marketplace pitch**: "neutral bankable subscription OS + regulated money movement for financeable infrastructure in LATAM"; wedge = C&I solar Mexico; fideicomiso 381 fuente-de-pago escrow rail; CFE-data scoring moat. VC-committee loop peaked ~89/100 (Team/GTM/Fundability cap ~89 without real docs).
