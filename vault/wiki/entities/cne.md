# CNE — Comisión Nacional de Energía

**Type:** organization (federal energy regulator)

Mexico's energy‑sector regulator under the 2025 framework. An **órgano administrativo desconcentrado, de carácter técnico, sectorizado a la [[sener|Secretaría de Energía]]**, with technical, operative, management, and decision independence (Ley de la Comisión Nacional de Energía, "LCNE"). Its object is to **regulate, supervise, and sanction** activities in energy matters, in line with the binding planning and the sectoral instruments SENER issues.

> **⚠ Authority resolved (2025).** Earlier wiki notes flagged CNE's powers as a known gap and treated [[cre]] as the permit/regulation authority. The [[2026-06-09-autoconsumo-cne-2025|CNE DACG de Autoconsumo (DOF 12‑XII‑2025)]] settles it: **CNE — not CRE — now grants, modifies, terminates, and supervises generation and storage permits** (LSE art. 151; LCNE arts. 7‑II, 8‑IV) and **issues the DACGs** governing those activities. CNE is the body a ≥ 0.7 MW [[autoconsumo]] project deals with.

## Role in this wiki

CNE is the issuing/permitting authority for the **2025 Autoconsumo regime**:
- Issued the **DACG de Autoconsumo** (DOF 12‑XII‑2025) and the **Acuerdo de requisitos 0.7–20 MW** (DOF 06‑VIII‑2025) and **Formato** (DOF 07‑X‑2025).
- **Grants the generation permit** for [[autoconsumo]] ≥ 0.7 MW (simplified for 0.7–20 MW interconnected; ordinary above), via the **OPE** (Oficialía de Partes Electrónica, ope.cne.gob.mx).
- Maintains the **Registro de Autoconsumo** (annual update, 1T each year).
- Expedites the **modelos de contrato** — interconnection, and the *Contrato de venta de Excedentes de Autoconsumo y Productos Asociados* (LSE art. 11).
- Owns **tariff calculation/adjustment methodology** for Suministro Básico (the role the GDMTH page already pointed to — see below).

## Key powers (LCNE / LSE)

- Expedir regulación y **DACGs** sobre las actividades en materia energética; supervisar y vigilar su cumplimiento (LCNE art. 7‑II).
- **Otorgar, modificar, terminar y supervisar** los permisos de generación y comercialización (LSE art. 151).
- Promover el desarrollo ordenado, continuo y seguro de la generación (LCNE art. 8‑VII).
- Emitir modelos de contrato de interconexión, conexión, y de compraventa de excedentes de Autoconsumo (LSE art. 11).
- Tariff methodology/adjustment for final Suministro Básico tariffs (per the GDMTH page contact note).

## Relationship to CRE and SENER

- **vs. [[cre]]:** under the LIE‑2014 framework, CRE issued generation/autoabastecimiento permits and the tariff/DG/storage DACGs. Under the 2025 LSE, that permit and DACG authority sits with **CNE**. CRE's continuing role (and whether its instruments like [[2026-06-04-acuerdo-a158-2024|A/158/2024]] remain valid or migrate to CNE) is the open tension — noted on [[cre]].
- **vs. [[sener]]:** CNE is *sectorized under* SENER and acts in line with SENER's binding planning and sectoral instruments (e.g. the [[2026-06-08-pdse-2025-2039|PDSE]]).
