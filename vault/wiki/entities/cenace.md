# CENACE — Centro Nacional de Control de Energía

**Type:** organization (independent system operator)

Mexico's independent electricity system operator. CENACE manages the real-time operation of the national grid and administers the wholesale electricity market (Mercado Eléctrico Mayorista, MEM). It is a decentralized public entity, operationally independent from CFE.

## Relevance to GDMTH billing

CENACE's operation cost appears as a pass-through billing component in [[gdmth]] and other final consumer tariffs. The charge is included in the integrated rates consumers pay — it is not separately itemized on a typical CFE bill but is part of the rate structure.

## Relevance to this wiki

CENACE is the grid operator whose market prices and dispatch decisions ultimately drive the energy and capacity cost components embedded in CFE's retail tariffs.
