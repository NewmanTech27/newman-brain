# ENEL México

**Type:** organization

Generator (Enel Green Power México / Enel Generación) supplying [[gepp]]'s **Proplasa** complex (PR1, PR2, Tapa — one predio, three meters, Valle de México Norte) under a **legacy autoabasto contract running to 2032**, ~60–70% of the site's consumption.

## Key attributes
- 2025 supply to Proplasa: 64.0 GWh of 92.0 GWh total (69.6%) — PR1 87%, PR2 57%, TAP 79%
- Price (workbook): **$1.78–1.92/kWh**, roughly CFE GDMTH intermedia level for the división
- **2026 anomaly:** share collapsed from ~55% (Ene–Feb) to ~16% (Mar–Abr); cause unknown — the balance shifted to CFE at higher effective cost

## Relevance to this wiki
The Proplasa "combine autoabasto + on-site PV" ask hinges on the same legacy-porteo + DG coexistence question as [[tala-energy]]/Ixtlahuacán. The 2026 supply collapse makes on-site generation more urgent for GEPP: every ENEL kWh lost is replaced at full CFE rates. See [[2026-06-10-gepp-portfolio-project-check]].
