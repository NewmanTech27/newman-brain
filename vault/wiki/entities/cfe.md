# CFE — Comisión Federal de Electricidad

**Type:** organization (state-owned enterprise)

Mexico's national electricity utility, owned by the federal government. CFE is the dominant electricity generator, transmitter, distributor, and supplier in Mexico. Under the current regulatory framework, CFE operates through several subsidiaries corresponding to the unbundled segments of the electricity market.

## Key subsidiaries relevant to this wiki

| Entity | Role |
|---|---|
| **[[cfe-ssb]]** (CFE Suministrador de Servicios Básicos) | Retail electricity supply to regulated customers; issues bills under tariffs like [[gdmth]] |
| **CFE Transmisión** | Operates the national transmission grid |
| **CFE Distribución** | Operates local distribution networks |
| **CFE Generación** | Generation assets |

## Regulatory context

- Electricity market regulated by **CRE** (Comisión Reguladora de Energía)
- Grid operation managed by **[[cenace]]** (Centro Nacional de Control de Energía)
- Tariff methodology overseen by **[[cne]]** (Comisión Nacional de Energía)
- Final consumer tariffs governed by Acuerdo A/158/2024 and related CRE resolutions

## Relevance to this wiki

CFE is the tariff authority and billing entity for all content in this wiki. Understanding CFE's structure, tariff categories, billing methodology, and regulatory framework is the core subject matter.
