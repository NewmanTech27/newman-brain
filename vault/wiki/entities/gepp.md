# GEPP

**Type:** organization

GEPP (Grupo GEPP) is PepsiCo's bottler in Mexico — a nationwide network of bottling, preform/cap (Proplasa) and beverage plants. As a CFE client it is a large multi-service GDMTH portfolio: ~30+ metered services across divisions, **411.7 GWh and $1,205M MXN in 2025** (client workbook figures).

## Key attributes
- Portfolio: 2025 = 411.7 GWh, $1,205.3M total recibos, punta share ~9.5%, portfolio FP cargo $4.83M
- Legacy autoabasto contracts (both vence 2032): [[tala-energy]] → Ixtlahuacán (~50% of consumption, zafra-seasonal); [[enel-mexico]] → Proplasa (~60–70%)
- Flagged sites in the 2026 brief: Ixtlahuacán (Jalisco, 5,000 kW), Acapulco (Centro Sur, 2,271 kW), Cancún (Peninsular, 1,500 kW), Proplasa (Valle de México Norte, 3 meters/one predio, 11,039 kW combined)
- Largest single consumer: Proplasa (92 GWh/yr combined)

## Relevance to this wiki
First multi-site portfolio brief. The asks map directly onto the 2025 regulatory stack: legacy-autoabasto + on-site PV coexistence ([[autoconsumo]], [[medicion-neta]]), on-site PPA via Grupo de Autoconsumo, abasto aislado feasibility, plus immediate FP-correction levers at Acapulco/Cancún.
