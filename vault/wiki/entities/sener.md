# SENER — Secretaría de Energía

**Type:** organization (Mexican federal energy ministry)

The federal cabinet ministry responsible for Mexico's national energy policy and planning.
SENER sits **above** the technical regulator in the stack: it sets policy and issues the
high‑level instruments, while the **[[cne|Comisión Nacional de Energía]]** — an órgano
desconcentrado **sectorized under SENER** — regulates energy activities, grants generation/
storage permits, and issues the DACGs (e.g. the 2025 [[autoconsumo]] regime), and
[[cenace]] operates the grid. [[cre]] held the regulator role under the LIE‑2014 era.
Distinct from [[cfe]] (the state utility SENER's policy governs).

## Key attributes
- Issues and updates **NOM-001-SEDE-2012**, the mandatory electrical installations code (see [[instalacion-pv-interconectada]], [[instalacion-bess]]).
- Issued the **Manual de Interconexión de Centrales <0.5 MW** (DOF 2016) that defines the DG connection procedure ([[interconexion-cre]]).
- Publishes the **Plan de Desarrollo del Sector Eléctrico (PDSE/PRODESEN)** — the national electricity sector plan ([[2026-06-08-pdse-2025-2039]]) that confirmed the DG ceiling at < 0.7 MW.
- The **2025 framework** — Ley del Sector Eléctrico (LSE) and its Reglamento (**RLSE**, DOF 03‑X‑2025) — sits under SENER's policy ambit; the [[cne]] (sectorized under SENER) operationalizes it via DACGs (e.g. [[2026-06-09-autoconsumo-cne-2025]]).
- Custodian of the **Bases del Mercado Eléctrico** and first Reglas del Mercado.

## Relevance to this wiki
SENER is the source authority behind three of our layers: the **physical install code**
(NOM-001-SEDE), the **interconnection process** (Manual <0.5 MW), and the **sector plan**
that fixes the DG capacity ceiling. Its instruments bound what a PV/BESS project may be and
how it connects, upstream of CRE's tariff/compensation rules.
