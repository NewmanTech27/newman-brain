# CRE — Comisión Reguladora de Energía

**Type:** organization (federal regulatory body — LIE‑2014 era)

Mexico's electricity and natural gas sector regulator under the **LIE‑2014** framework. An autonomous technical regulatory body ("Órgano Regulador Coordinado en Materia Energética") with legal personality and technical, operational, and management independence. Governed the generation, transmission, distribution, and commercialization of electricity, and protected end‑user interests.

> **⚠ Superseded for permits (2025).** Under the 2025 Ley del Sector Eléctrico, **generation/storage permitting and the new DACGs moved to the [[cne]]** (an órgano desconcentrado of [[sener]]). The instruments below were issued by CRE under LIE‑2014 and remain the wiki's references for *what the rules say*, but the **authority** for new permits and autoconsumo regulation is now CNE. See [[2026-06-09-autoconsumo-cne-2025]].

## Role in this wiki

CRE issued most of the legacy regulatory instruments referenced in this wiki (now read as LIE‑2014‑era rules):
- [[gdmth]] tariff rules and the Acuerdos that define them (including [[2026-06-04-acuerdo-a158-2024|A/158/2024]])
- [[2026-06-04-dacg-sae-a113-2024|SAE regulation (A/113/2024)]]
- [[2026-06-04-res142-2017-gen-dist|Distributed generation rules (RES/142/2017)]]

## Key powers (LIE‑2014 era — several now migrated to CNE)

- Set methodology and rules for final consumer tariffs *(tariff methodology/adjustment now attributed to [[cne]])*
- Approved interconnection rules and contract models *(autoconsumo interconnection/contract models now [[cne]])*
- ~~Issued permits for electricity generation~~ → **now [[cne]]** (LSE art. 151)
- Oversaw [[cfe-ssb]] and other suministradores
- Reviewed and approved CENACE's market rules and operational procedures
- Issued DACGs *(new energy‑activity DACGs now [[cne]])*

## Governance

- Board: 5 Comisionados, appointed by the President, ratified by the Senate
- Chair: Comisionado Presidente
- As of the A/113/2024 issuance (September 2024): Comisionado Presidente Leopoldo Vicente Melchi García

## Legal basis under the 2025 reform

Under the [[2026-06-04-ley-sector-electrico-2025|2025 Ley del Sector Eléctrico]] and the Ley de la CNE, the **[[cne|Comisión Nacional de Energía]]** is the regulator that grants generation/storage permits and issues energy‑activity DACGs (LSE art. 151; LCNE art. 7‑II). The 2025 DACGs are all CNE instruments, not CRE.

> **Open tension — validity of legacy CRE instruments.** Whether CRE's still‑cited tariff and DG/storage instruments — [[2026-06-04-acuerdo-a158-2024|A/158/2024]] (FC=0.57, period schedules, demand formulas), [[2026-06-04-dacg-sae-a113-2024|A/113/2024]] (SAE‑CC), [[2026-06-04-res142-2017-gen-dist|RES/142/2017]] (medición neta) — remain in force as‑is, or migrate to CNE, is **not yet confirmed**. The wiki continues to rely on their *content* (it is the best available rule text) while flagging the authority question. Noted reciprocally on [[cne]].
