# Plan de Desarrollo del Sector Eléctrico 2025-2039 (PDSE)

**Type:** official government plan (ACUERDO SENER)
**Date:** Signed 14 Oct 2025; published DOF 17 Oct 2025
**Author:** Secretaría de Energía (SENER) — Mtra. Luz Elena González Escobar
**Source:** DOF nota 5770297 — https://www.dof.gob.mx/nota_detalle_popup.php?codigo=5770297
**Raw file:** `raw/articles/DOF - Diario Oficial de la Federación.md`

## Summary

SENER's 15-year planning document for the national electricity sector, mandated by the Ley de Planeación y Transición Energética (DOF 18-mar-2025). It is the rectoral policy document for generation, transmission, and distribution development through 2039. For this wiki its decisive contribution is a single authoritative sentence that resolves the open question on the distributed-generation capacity ceiling.

## Key claims

- **DG ceiling is now < 0.7 MW.** §2.2.3: *"Los sistemas GD se caracterizan por ser de pequeña escala; en la regulación actual se establece una capacidad máxima de menos de 0.7 MW (artículo 25 de la Ley del Sector Eléctrico)."* This supersedes the prior 0.5 MW (500 kW) limit from RES/142/2017.
- **2024 GD capacity:** 4,449.79 MW cumulative installed, >99.4% solar PV.
- **GD-FV detail:** >405,000 contracts, 4,423 MW, 6,777 GWh generated in 2024; GCR Occidental holds 36.5% of capacity.
- DG defined as generation from plants that **do not require a permit** and are interconnected to circuits with high load-center concentration.
- Legal basis chain: Ley del Sector Eléctrico (DOF 18-mar-2025) + Ley de Planeación y Transición Energética + its Reglamento (DOF 3-oct-2025).

## Contradictions / tensions

- **Supersedes** the 0.5 MW DG limit in [[2026-06-04-res142-2017-gen-dist|RES/142/2017]] and [[generacion-distribuida]] (as originally written). Noted on both pages.
- The source confirms the *capacity ceiling* (art. 25 Ley del Sector Eléctrico) but does **not** restate the compensation mechanics (medición neta credit rules), which remain governed by RES/142/2017 until shown otherwise.
