# IEA-PVPS T17-04:2025 — PV-Powered Electric Vehicle Charging Stations

**Type:** research report (IEA PVPS Task 17 — PV and Transport)
**Date:** January 2025
**Author:** Sechilariu, Alchami, Cheikh-Mohamad, Robisson, Brito
**Raw file:** `raw/pdfs/2025-07-02 - IEA-PVPS-T17-04-2025-REPORT-PV powered Charging-Stations.pdf` (136 pp)

## Summary
Requirements, barriers, solutions, and social acceptance for **PV-powered EV charging
stations (PVCS)**: combining PV + battery + power management to charge EVs, with attention
to **grid/DSO interaction and demand response**, real-time power-management optimization
(MILP), human-system interfaces, cost & carbon (LCA) methodology, and case studies. An
**adjacent opportunity** to our core CFE PV+BESS work — the same PV+battery+EMS stack
([[hybrid-inverters]], [[bess]]) pointed at EV load instead of, or alongside, demand
reduction. Relevant for hospitality clients (Posadas) adding guest EV charging.

## Key claims
- PVCS = PV + battery + smart power management; **demand-response and DSO coordination** are central (parallels GDMTH demand-charge management — [[bess-savings-model]]).
- Real-time optimization (MILP) balances PV, storage, grid, and EV demand — same control problem class as BESS peak-shaving dispatch.
- Includes a **global cost & carbon (LCA) assessment methodology** for PVCS.
- Social acceptance / HMI matters for adoption (case study France).
