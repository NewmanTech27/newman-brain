# Tarifa GDMTH — Gran Demanda en Media Tensión Horaria

## Summary

This is the official CFE specification for the **GDMTH tariff** (Gran Demanda en Media Tensión Horaria) — the time-of-use electricity tariff applied to medium-voltage commercial and industrial consumers with demand ≥ 100 kW. It is one of the main tariff categories under CFE's Suministro Básico (SSB) offering.

The tariff uses **three time periods** — punta, intermedio, and base — defined differently across 8 tariff regions and two seasons. This creates a matrix of 8 regions × 2 seasons × 3 day types (weekday/Saturday/Sunday-holiday) of applicable hours. Energy is billed separately per period; demand is billed via two distinct charges: capacity (based on peak-coincident demand) and distribution (based on max monthly demand), both capped by a load-factor formula.

The source captures tariff rules and period schedules in full, but **does not contain the actual numeric rate values** ($/kWh and $/kW-mes) — these appear to be dynamically loaded content the web clipper did not capture.

## Key claims

- Applies to medium-voltage supply with demand ≥ 100 kW
- Billing components: energy (punta + intermedio + base kWh) + capacity charge ($/kW-mes) + distribution charge ($/kW-mes) + basic supplier operation charge
- Contracted demand: minimum of 60% of total connected load, 100 kW, or capacity of largest motor/device installed
- Capacity charge demand = min(Dmax_punta, Qmensual / (d × FC × 24))
- Distribution charge demand = min(Dmax_mensual, Qmensual / (d × FC × 24))
- Demand measured in 15-minute intervals; fractional kW rounded up
- Security deposit = 2× (capacity charge × contracted demand in kW)
- Users who maintain <100 kW for 12 consecutive months may request reclassification to [[gdmto]] tariff
- Rules and load factors reference **Acuerdo A/158/2024**, Annex sections 3.1.2 and 3.1.5
- For autoabastecimiento users: only CFE SSB-supplied demand/consumption is used in calculations
