## Summary

GDMTO is the non-time-of-use (ordinaria) CFE tariff for medium-voltage commercial and industrial loads **below 100 kW**. It is the structural predecessor to [[gdmth]]: users who exceed 100 kW of measured demand for three consecutive months must migrate to GDMTH or risk contract rescission.

The tariff uses the same umbral demand-cap formula as GDMTH (same Acuerdo A/158/2024 reference), but because there are no time-of-use periods, the Cargo por Capacidad collapses to the umbral alone — no punta demand to measure. The Cargo por Distribución retains the min(Dmax_mensual, umbral) structure. FC for GDMTO is 0.55 (vs 0.57 for GDMTH).

Rate values ($/kWh and $/kW-mes) are not included in the clipped page — they are image-based tables on the CFE portal and require separate capture.

## Key claims

- Application: any medium-voltage supply with demand **< 100 kW**, any use
- No time-of-use periods; energy billed at a single flat rate
- **Capacidad demand:** if no punta period (or no demand metering) → billed demand = `Qmensual / (d × FC × 24)` only (umbral, no min())
- **Distribución demand:** `min(Dmax_mensual, umbral)` — same two-component structure as GDMTH
- FC = **0.55** (from A/158/2024 §3.1.2)
- Contracted demand minimum: max(60% of connected load, 10 kW, capacity of largest motor)
- If 60% connected load > substation capacity: contracted demand = substation kVA × 90%
- Demand measured in 15-min intervals; fractions rounded up to full kW
- Deposit: 2× (capacity charge rate × contracted demand kW)
- **Migration trigger:** measured demand ≥ 100 kW for 3 consecutive months → must request GDMTH; CFE may rescind contract
- **Autoabastecimiento users:** Qmensual = CFE SSB-supplied kWh only (self-generated excluded from umbral)

## Contradictions / tensions

None identified. GDMTO explicitly cites Acuerdo A/158/2024 §3.1.2 for FC values, consistent with the same citation on GDMTH. The fallback formula (umbral-only for capacidad) is consistent with what the GDMTH page says about days with no punta period.
