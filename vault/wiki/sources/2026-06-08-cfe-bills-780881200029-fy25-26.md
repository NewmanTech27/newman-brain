## Summary

A complete 12-month set of GDMTH bills for a large Grupo Posadas property in Cancún. Unlike the single anomalous [[2026-06-07-cfe-bill-795150706028-mar26|hotel bill]] (post-collapse, 12 kW), this is a **stable, high-consumption industrial-scale load** (~700k–1,000k kWh/month, peak demands 1,400–2,000 kW) — a clean basis for a yearly savings model. This set is the input for the savings analysis [[2026-06-08-780881200029-yearly-savings]] and is cross-checked against the client's manual Excel model ([[2026-06-08-calculadora-bess-pv-excel]]).

**Primary value:** (1) 12 months of period-resolved consumption + real SIN rates; (2) independent validation of the Excel transcription (accurate to the peso); (3) empirical confirmation of the distribución billing base.

- **Annual baseline (sin IVA): $30,157,371.** Demand charges (capacidad + distribución) ≈ $9.5M/yr (~31%); energy ≈ $20.6M/yr (~68%).
- Billed **capacidad** = `kW Punta` (none capped — umbral never binds; punta demand < piso every month).
- Billed **distribución** = `max(kW B, I, P)` capped by umbral (also never binds).

## Key claims

- The site is **energy-dominated by intermedio** (~60% of kWh) and base (~31%); punta is only ~9% of kWh but demand-charge-heavy.
- **Punta demand is consistently below the umbral**, so all demand is billed on measured peaks, not the cap — meaning peak shaving (BESS) translates directly to capacidad savings, with no umbral floor to fight.
- SIN region: solar produces essentially **zero kWh during punta** (punta 18:00–22:00 winter / 20:00–22:00 summer) — PV savings land in intermedio, not punta. See [[horarios-y-divisiones]].

## Contradictions / tensions

- **Distribución billing base:** the printed `KWMax` register exceeds `max(period demands)` in ABR/OCT/DIC, yet CFE billed distribución on the lower `max(period demands)` in all three. This **corrects** [[demanda-facturable]] and [[gdmth-bill-structure]], which previously said distribución uses `Dmax_mensual = KWMax`. (See correction box on [[demanda-facturable]].)
- **Excel transcription:** the client's [[2026-06-08-calculadora-bess-pv-excel|Excel]] recorded `kW Máxima` as `max(B,I,P)` (correct for billing) rather than the printed `KWMax` — so its baseline is correct despite not matching the `KWMax` field.
