## Errors (quantified)

- **E1 — FP bonificación never transcribed.** `TARIFF_RATES` column M ("Bon/Carg FP") is empty; `BASELINE_COST` column H = 0 every month. The site runs FP at **99.5–99.98%**, earning a ~1.2% bonificación monthly (−$24,028 to −$32,559; −$356,110/yr, from the PDFs via the engine extractor). The Excel's "unexplained +1.2% systematic overshoot" (flagged in [[2026-06-08-780881200029-yearly-savings]] §5) is **exactly this, to the centavo** (Abr: diff $34,059.13 = bonif $29,361.57 × 1.16). Mystery closed.
- **E2 — Arbitrage physically impossible.** `DISPATCH!F92` = daily discharge × **calendar days**: the battery cycles 365 days/yr, but **punta only exists on weekdays** (SIN, [[horarios-y-divisiones]]); and no cap vs the bill's punta energy — April credits 82,961 kWh of punta displacement when the **entire bill holds 40,790 punta kWh**. Corrected (Mon–Fri minus festivos, capped at punta kWh): discharge 1,009,362 → **670,200 kWh/yr (−34%)**, arbitrage $1,216,238 → **$790,049 (−35%)**. Side effect: ~242 cycles/yr, not 365 — battery life assumption improves.
- **E3 — Bonificación claw-back missing.** The bonificación is proportional to the bill; savings shrink the bill, so ~1.2% of every savings peso is clawed back: **−$84,528/yr**.
- **E4 — O&M PV escalation exponent bug.** `L64 = O&M × (1+esc)^(C64−1)` uses the escalation **factor** (1.06) as exponent instead of the year: O&M PV grows ~0.35%/yr instead of 6%/yr (year-15 cost $65,400 vs correct $136,343 — financier flows overstated late-term).
- **E5 — Falla factor applied to O&M.** `I64 = 0.75 × (BESS savings − O&M BESS)`: the 3-months-down assumption also waives 25% of the O&M cost. Downtime doesn't pause O&M contracts.

## Inconsistencies & traceability failures

- **I1 — Unlabeled perspectives.** The headline TIR/VPN/payback are the **financier's** (IRR over `Q63:Q83`), shown without saying so; the client's 20-yr number ($98.8M) sits in the same table. Violates the financial-reporting doctrine (financier IRR is deal-specific). v1 never computes the unlevered project IRR at all.
- **I2 — Two "Ahorro %" definitions:** `G12` = 25.18% (totals ratio) vs `M29` = 24.92% (average of monthly percentages). Both displayed.
- **I3 — Two paybacks** (6 = undiscounted, 9 = discounted marker in scratch), neither labeled.
- **I4 — "Ahorro Bruto Año 1" is year-0.** `F12` shows unescalated monthly-table totals; the projection's year 1 is a different number ($5.78M after falla+escalation).
- **I5 — Frozen literal.** `DISPATCH!M108 = 6595448.462662447` is a **hardcoded copy** of a formula intermediate, feeding the 20-yr projection's year-1 BESS savings. Change any input and the projection silently desyncs from the monthly model.
- **I6 — Dead scratch everywhere.** `P108:R122` block (alternative blended-rate savings calc, `$1.9/kWh` magic constant), `M106/N106`, BCS branches on every formula for a SIN site, an entire BCS hourly matrix, two 378-row leasing sheets wired to an inactive toggle (`P60="No"`), and a `DISPATCH_ISLAND` mode.
- **I7 — Decorative hourly model.** Sections C/C2 (typical-day dispatch) don't feed the monthly aggregation — the monthly model uses the hour-divided flat shave instead. Two parallel BESS models, one of them dead weight that *looks* load-bearing.
- **I8 — Wrong industry profile.** `Tipo de Industria = Comercial/Retail` for a **hotel** (the Hotel/Turismo column exists unused); the synthetic curve puts ~2× the real punta energy in the window (modeled 3,570 kWh/day vs the bill's ~1,854) and peaks at noon while the bills peak in the evening. This is what made E2 look plausible.
- **I9 — Magic numbers in formulas:** fianza 13.9% (`M64 = N64*0.139`), the 1.9 $/kWh in scratch, `CEILING(...,1)` rounding the umbral.
