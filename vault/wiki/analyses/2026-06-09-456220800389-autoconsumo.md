# Autoconsumo / Autoabastecimiento for 456220800389

**Question:** (1) Would moving the project from medición neta to an autoconsumo
contraprestación change savings? (2) Could scaling PV above the 0.7 MW Generador Exento
ceiling — crossing into autoconsumo/autoabastecimiento legislation — produce higher savings?

## Site facts that drive the answer
- Tariff GDMTH/SIN; **demand-bound**, flat 24/7 industrial load. Annual consumption 5.58 GWh;
  PV 700 kWp generates 1.28 GWh = **23% of load, ~fully self-consumed** (~zero export).
- Billed demand `max(B,I,P)` falls in **intermedio (daytime) every month** (~903–967 kW), but
  base (~850–907) and punta (~767–904) sit only ~50 kW below it — a flat profile.
- Charge stack: **capacidad $4.34M/yr** (on kW_punta), **distribución $1.85M/yr** (on max(B,I,P)),
  plus energía/generación. SIN punta is after sunset → **PV = 0 at punta**.

## Q1 — Switching contraprestación (medición neta → facturación neta): NEUTRAL
Facturación neta differs from [[medicion-neta]] only in surplus treatment (monthly PML cash vs
12-mo credit carryforward). This site has **no meaningful surplus** (midday load > PV output),
so there is nothing to settle differently and no PML-expiry risk under medición neta either.
→ **Savings identical (~$5.18M/yr w/ FP).** The project already operates in physical
"autoconsumo" mode ([[autoconsumo]]).

## Q2 — Autoabastecimiento demand-netting: ~no prize here
Under autoabastecimiento, demand bills on **CFE-supplied (net) quantities** ([[autoconsumo]] §1,
[[demanda-facturable]]). But for this site:
- **Capacidad ($4.34M, the big charge) is immune to PV at any size** — PV = 0 during SIN punta.
  Only the BESS shaves it. No scheme changes this.
- **Distribución netting is floored.** PV could net against the daytime intermedio peak, but the
  flat base/punta demand (~890 kW) caps the reduction at
  Σ(intermedio − max(base,punta)) ≈ 649 kW-mo × ~$165 ≈ **~$0.1M/yr best case** — not worth a
  CRE permit.

## Q2b — Scaling PV above 0.7 MW: real but energy-only, and self-consumption-capped
The value of crossing the ceiling is **not** the scheme's demand-netting — it is simply removing
the cap so you can **self-consume more energy at retail value** (~$1.97/kWh effective, the
PV-only rate; ~3.5-yr payback per incremental kWp). Bounds:
- **Headroom** to ~**1.0–1.2 MWp** before solar-noon output overruns midday load (~700–940 kW);
  the 1,700 kWh BESS extends this by soaking midday PV (free) for punta discharge — a genuine
  synergy that also improves arbitrage (today BESS charges from paid grid base energy).
- **Beyond the self-consumption ceiling**, incremental PV exports at **PML (~half retail)** —
  poor, and the export price is unconfirmed under the 2025 Ley.
- **Capacidad never improves** — the structural ceiling on PV value here.
- Illustrative: +300–500 kWp self-consumed ≈ **+$1.0–1.5M/yr** at ~4-yr incremental payback
  (CAPEX ~$13,162/kWp). **Assumption-based, not engine-validated.**

### Engine limitation
`tools/cfe_savings` values **all** PV at retail (assumes 100% self-consumption); it has no
export/PML model. So PV > ~700 kWp cannot be sized honestly without a **15-min interval load
profile** to find the self-consumption ceiling and export fraction. Do not run the engine at
1.5 MWp and quote the result — it overstates.

## Costs / risks of crossing 0.7 MW
- Exit permit-free Generador Exento → **CRE generation/autoabastecimiento permit** (6–18 mo,
  interconnection study). 2025 Ley self-supply + export rules **not yet confirmed**
  ([[autoconsumo]], [[generacion-distribuida]], [[medicion-neta]]).
- Interconnection/hosting: contracted demand 1,004 kW; ~1.2 MWp (~950 kW AC) bumps the 80%
  hosting rule ([[interconexion-cre]]).

## Answer
- **Switching schemes alone: no gain** (neutral contraprestación; demand-netting floored).
- **Scaling PV above 0.7 MW: plausibly +$1–1.5M/yr**, but **entirely energy-side**, capped by
  daytime self-consumption (~1.0–1.2 MWp), with diminishing PML returns beyond, and only if you
  accept the permit + 2025-Ley risk. Capacidad — the biggest charge — is untouched at any size.
- **Recommended:** keep PV 700 kWp + BESS + FP correction under medición neta unless 15-min data
  confirms enough daytime absorption to justify the permit jump.
