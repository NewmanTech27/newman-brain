# Tala Energy

**Type:** organization

Cogenerator that produces electricity from **sugarcane bagasse** (ingenio at Tala, Jalisco) and supplies [[gepp]]'s Ixtlahuacán plant under a **legacy autoabasto contract running to 2032** (LIE/LSP-era figure, porteo through CFE's network). Because the fuel is bagasse, generation is **zafra-seasonal: it covers ~100% of Ixtlahuacán's consumption Dec–May and ~0% Jun–Nov** (~50% annual).

## Key attributes
- Billing model (per the GEPP workbook): CFE-equivalent GDMTH rates, then a **10% descuento** on importe de energía ($2.68M discount on Ixtlahuacán in 2025)
- Contract: ~50% of Ixtlahuacán's annual consumption, vence 2032
- The workbook lists ~11 other "Plantas TALA" (Tuxtla, Puebla, Veracruz, Morelia, Celaya, Edosa, Uruapan, Orizaba, Villahermosa, Tepic) — data broken (`#REF!`) in our copy

## Relevance to this wiki
The seasonal gap (Jun–Nov on full CFE) is exactly where on-site PV adds most value at Ixtlahuacán; during zafra months PV displaces Tala kWh at only ~90% of CFE rates. Whether the legacy porteo contract can coexist with a [[medicion-neta]] interconnection is an open gap ([[overview]]).
