# NOM-009-STPS-2011 — Condiciones de Seguridad para Trabajos en Altura

**Type:** Norma Oficial Mexicana (STPS — labor/occupational safety)
**Date:** DOF 6 May 2011 (modifies NOM-009-STPS-1999)
**Author:** Secretaría del Trabajo y Previsión Social
**Raw file:** `raw/pdfs/2025-07-01 - Secretaria de trabajo y prevension social NOM-009-STPS-2011.pdf`

## Summary
The mandatory Mexican occupational-safety standard for **work at height (>1.80 m above the
reference level)** — directly applicable to **rooftop PV installation**, where crews mount
modules ([[pv-modules]]) and run conduit on resort/commercial roofs. It is the **labor-law
layer** of a project, parallel to the electrical install code ([[instalacion-pv-interconectada]],
NOM-001-SEDE) and the interconnection process ([[interconexion-cre]]). Compliance is an
EPC/installer obligation that shows up in project cost, schedule, and liability — and a
Unidad de Inspección or client HSE audit can check it.

## Key claims
- **Trabajos en altura = activities >1.80 m** above the reference surface (incl. openings, shafts, pits).
- **Patrón (employer) obligations**: prior hazard analysis of the work area; written work authorization for scaffolds/suspended platforms/lifts; Spanish-language manufacturer manuals/procedures; supervision; inspection & maintenance program with ≥1-year records; provide a **personal fall-arrest system** (per NOM-017-STPS) + PPE; medical exams; emergency plan; first-aid kit; training; supervise contractors.
- **Fall-arrest system components** defined: arnés (full-body harness), absorbedor de energía, línea de vida (horizontal/vertical), conector, punto de anclaje, bloqueador de caída.
- **Collective protection**: barandales/protección lateral and redes de seguridad at roof edges and openings.
- Covers scaffolds (tipo torre, suspendidos), elevating platforms, ladders, safety nets.
- References sister NOMs: 004 (machine guards), 017 (PPE), 026 (signage), 029 (electrical-installation maintenance).

## Entities mentioned
- STPS — issuing labor authority; its inspectors (autoridad laboral) enforce
- the *Patrón* (EPC/installer employing the crew) — bears the compliance burden

## Concepts mentioned
- [[instalacion-pv-interconectada]] — the electrical-install counterpart on the same roof
- [[pv-modules]] — the physical modules being lifted/mounted at height
- [[protection-bos]] — conduit/cable runs installed under the same conditions

## Contradictions / tensions
None identified — this is an additive labor-safety layer, no conflict with electrical/tariff rules.
