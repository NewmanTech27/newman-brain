# Acuerdo A/158/2024 — CRE Tarifas Finales Suministro Básico 2025

**Type:** regulation (CRE administrative act)  
**Date:** Approved 2024-12-13; in force 2025-01-01 through 2025-12-31  
**Authority:** [[cre]] (Comisión Reguladora de Energía)  
**Raw file:** `raw/pdfs/Acuerdo A 158 2024.pdf`

## Summary

The CRE's annual authorizing act for [[cfe-ssb]] tariff methodology and final rates for all Suministro Básico users during calendar year 2025. It defines the complete calculation and adjustment mechanism for the 12 tariff categories across 17 tariff divisions in Mexico.

The Acuerdo has two main parts: the resolution text (articles PRIMERO through DECIMOQUINTO) and the Annexo Único, which is the operative technical document. The Annexo contains the full billing methodology: tariff structure, generation cost components, load factors, diversity factors, period schedules, and application criteria — including the capacity and distribution demand cap formulas.

This is the **authoritative source** for all GDMTH billing parameters. It supersedes A/073/2023 (2024 rates) and remains in force until modified or replaced.

## Key claims

- GDMTH **factor de carga (FC) = 0.57** (Table 2, Section 3.1.2)
- GDMTH **factor de diversidad (FD) = 1.2** (Table 3)
- Capacity charge demand formula: `min(Dmax_punta, Q / (d × FC × 24))` (Section 5.1)
- Distribution charge demand formula: `min(Dmax_mensual, Q / (d × FC × 24))` (Section 5.2)
- Period schedules for GDMTH defined for 3 interconnected systems: BC, BCS, SIN (Table 5)
- All 15 mainland divisions use SIN schedule; BC and BCS use their own separate schedules
- Tariff category GDMTH = Gran Demanda **≥ 100 kW** en Media Tensión Horaria (Table 1)
- **17 tariff divisions** (not 8 regions) — mapping by municipality in Tables 25–42
- SCnMEM rate = **$0.0062/kWh** (Acuerdo article PRIMERO)
- Generation costs are updated **monthly** by CRE; the Acuerdo sets the methodology and initial estimates

## Contradictions / tensions

The Acuerdo is issued under the LIE 2014 framework (references LIE articles 138, 139). The March 2025 Ley del Sector Eléctrico replaced the LIE; CRE's continued authority under the new law is flagged in [[2026-06-04-ley-sector-electrico-2025]]. The A/158/2024 was approved December 2024 (before the new law) and remains operationally in force.
