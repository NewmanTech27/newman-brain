# NREL — Best Practices in Commercial & Industrial (C&I) PV System Installation

**Type:** research report (NREL / Solar Access to Public Capital working group)
**Date:** December 2015 (NREL/SR-6A20-65286)
**Author:** Doyle, Loomans, Truitt, Lockhart, Golden, Dabbagh, Lawrence (NABCEP); NREL monitors
**Raw file:** `raw/pdfs/2025-07-02 - Best practices in Commercial and Industrial C&I PV System Installation.pdf` (48 pp)

## Summary
An industry-consensus installation protocol for **C&I PV** assembled by NREL with a broad
set of installers, financiers (S&P, kWh Analytics), and NABCEP. Its explicit purpose is to
**mitigate risk and improve the reliability of PV asset energy and cash-flow production** —
i.e., a bankability/standardization document, not just a how-to. Directly relevant to the
EPC quality layer behind our project models and the SaaS/leasing financials, and complements
the Mexican code stack ([[instalacion-pv-interconectada]], [[2026-06-08-nom-009-stps-2011]]).

## Key claims
- Standardized install practices → more predictable energy yield and cash flow → better financing terms (the report was built with capital providers in the room).
- Targets the C&I segment specifically (between residential and utility) — our exact market.
- Consensus across installers + financiers + certifiers (NABCEP) gives it weight as a quality benchmark.
