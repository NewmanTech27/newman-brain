## Key observations

- **Demand collapse after Sep 2025:** Peak demand dropped from 82 kW (Sep 25) to 16–23 kW (Oct 25 – Mar 26). Energy dropped from ~38–53k kWh/month to 6–9k kWh/month. Cause unknown — renovation, partial closure, or sub-metering change.
- **SIN punta problem for PV:** In the SIN region (Campeche), punta hours are 20:00–22:00 (summer) and 18:00–22:00 (winter). Solar generation ends by ~18:00–19:00. Essentially **zero PV generation falls in punta hours**. PV savings are mostly in intermedia (kWh rate $1.887/kWh), not punta.
- **BESS opportunity for demand:** Demand charge dominates the bill when consumption is low (capacidad + distribución = $5,814/mes vs $12,935 energy). A BESS could shave even 12 kW during the 2-hour punta window.
- **Factor de potencia excellence:** FP = 99.96% earns the bonificación (−$489.65) — no reactive power issue here.
