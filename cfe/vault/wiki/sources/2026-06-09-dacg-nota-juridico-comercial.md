## Summary

A jurídico-comercial brief that distills the CNE Autoconsumo regulatory package (DOF 12‑XII‑2025 and companions) into the decisions a developer/EPC actually makes. It frames three things: (1) the **0.5 → 0.7 MW** jump in the exempt threshold, (2) the **simplified permit** for 0.7–20 MW interconnected projects, and (3) the **continuous‑compliance obligations** plus a **project‑development roadmap**. It is the practical lens over the dense DACG/RLSE/LSE text.

## Key claims

- **Generación exenta raised to 0.7 MW.** Régimen anterior (LIE 2014): 0.5 MW. Régimen vigente (LSE / DACG 2025): **0.7 MW**. Impact: installations **< 0.7 MW** need *no permit, no registro, no CNE obligation* (Art. 30 LSE; Num. 1.7‑I & 2.1 DACG). See [[generacion-distribuida]], [[generador-exento]].
- **Simplified vs. ordinary permit.** 0.7–20 MW interconnected → *trámite simplificado* (Num. 2.2 DACG + Acuerdo 06‑VIII‑2025): reduced documentation, **CENACE studies scoped to the point of interconnection**, est. **6–12 mo**. >20 MW or aislado ≥0.7 MW → *ordinario* (Num. 2.3 DACG + Disposiciones de Permisos 23‑X‑2025): full‑SEN studies, est. **12–24 mo**. Ventanilla: OPE — ope.cne.gob.mx.
- **Régimen comparison (< 0.7 / 0.7–20 / >20 MW):** permit (no / simplified / ordinary); interconnection contract (—/required/required); CENACE studies (n‑a / limited / full‑SEN); MEM registration (no / faltantes+excedentes / yes); excedentes sale (n‑a / only to CFE / only to CFE); annual Registro de Usuarias (n‑a / yes 1T / yes); intermittent backup (n‑a / SAE‑or‑CFE / SAE‑or‑CFE); termination on under‑use (n‑a / <50% conv & <30% renov over 12 mo / same).
- **Continuous obligations:** min‑use (Num. 1.6‑I), excedentes exclusivity to CFE (Art. 32‑II LSE; Num. 4.6), intermittent‑backup via SAE or CFE (Num. 4.5; Art. 59 RLSE), annual Registro (Art. 52 RLSE; Cap. VII), Redes Particulares independence (Num. 2.8).
- **Hoja de ruta (simplified regime):** (1) diagnóstico energético 2–4 sem → (2) estructuración jurídico‑comercial (EPC/PPA/arrendamiento/autodesarrollo; constitución del Grupo de Autoconsumo) 1–2 mo → (3) solicitud de permiso CNE 3–6 mo → (4) Estudios CENACE + Contrato de Interconexión (paralelo) → (5) construcción + COD ante CENACE → (6) operación + cumplimiento continuo.
