# NOM-001-SEDE-2012 — Instalaciones Eléctricas (Utilización)

**Type:** regulation / technical standard  
**Date:** 2012 (published DOF 2012; reviewed 2019 — version in vault)  
**Authority:** SENER — Secretaría de Energía  
**Raw file:** `raw/pdfs/NOM-001-SEDE-2012 (1).pdf` (780 pages)

## Summary

Mexico's national mandatory standard for electrical installations at the utilization level. It is Mexico's equivalent of NFPA 70 (U.S. National Electrical Code). Published by SENER, it applies to all industrial, commercial, and residential electrical installations in Mexico. The 2012 version incorporated the NEC 2008 and 2011 editions as reference for wiring methods.

The standard is organized into 10 Titles and 10 Chapters. The chapters relevant to PV/BESS/grid-tie installations are:

- **Artículo 690** — Sistemas Solares Fotovoltaicos (solar PV systems)
- **Artículo 480** — Baterías de Acumuladores (stationary battery storage)
- **Artículo 705** — Fuentes de Generación de Energía Eléctrica Interconectadas (grid-tied sources)
- **Artículo 445** — Generadores
- **Artículo 692** — Sistemas de Celdas de Combustible
- **Artículo 694** — Sistemas Eléctricos Eólicos Pequeños

## Key claims

- All electrical installations must comply with this NOM regardless of which other permits or CRE regulations apply. It is the baseline technical code.
- Grid-tied PV/BESS inverters must be "approved and identified for interconnection service" (705-4) — meaning certified equipment; field-assembled systems are not allowed.
- Installation of grid-tied sources must be done "only by qualified personnel" (705-6); same for PV (690-4(e)).
- The **120% bus rule** (705-12(d)(2)): the sum of OCPD ratings feeding a busbar cannot exceed 120% of the busbar's ampacity. This is a hard constraint on how much PV/BESS capacity can be added before a panel upgrade is required.
- **Anti-islanding** (705-40): upon loss of the primary source (grid), all generation sources must auto-disconnect. Certified interactive inverters may cease output instead of disconnecting conductors, and may auto-reconnect when grid returns.
- Battery systems (Art. 480 + 690-H) require ventilation, dedicated disconnects for >50V systems, and specific isolation by chemistry and voltage tier.

## Contradictions / tensions

The NOM governs *how* to install; CRE regulations (A/113/2024, RES/142/2017) govern *whether* to install and how to bill. These are parallel frameworks that must both be satisfied. No contradiction identified — they address different layers.

The NOM 2012 predates the 2025 Ley del Sector Eléctrico ([[2026-06-04-ley-sector-electrico-2025]]) and the 2024 CRE resolutions. Its technical requirements remain in force; only the regulatory/tariff layer has changed.
