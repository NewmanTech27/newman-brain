# CFE Bills — Service 456220800389 (Tototlán, Jalisco) May25–Apr26

**Raw files:** `raw/bills/456220800389/XC-*.xml` (12 CFDI XML files)

## Summary
Twelve consecutive monthly CFE bills for an industrial service in **Tototlán, Jalisco**
(CP 47730), delivered as **CFDI XML** (SAT cfd/4 with the iTComplements CFE complemento)
rather than PDF. Tariff **HM (GDMTH)**, división `DX` = Jalisco ([[horarios-y-divisiones|SIN]]
system). Contracted demand 1,004 kW; ~5.58 GWh/yr; max measured demand 905–967 kW. The bills
parse and foot cleanly; they feed the savings analysis [[2026-06-09-456220800389-yearly-savings]].

To parse these, an XML loader (`parse_bill_xml`) was added to the engine's `extract.py`;
raw bytes never enter LLM context.

## Key claims (from the bills)
- Annual baseline (sin IVA, Σ MEM importes) = **$15,620,783**; annual consumption 5,578,967 kWh.
- All 12 bills foot to within 0.000%: `(Σ MEM importes + cargo FP) × 1.16 = Facturación del
  Periodo`. No CFE arithmetic errors.
- **Power factor 85.7–87.5% every month** (< 90%) → recurring *cargo por bajo factor de
  potencia* (~$70k/month). A correctable, currently-unmodeled savings opportunity.
- Umbral (FC=0.57) ~990–1,191 kW exceeds max demand every month → **demand-bound site**
  (umbral never binds).
