# Generador Exento

A legal designation in Mexico's electricity sector for operators of small generation plants (**< 0.7 MW**) that are **exempt** from the full market participation requirements — they don't need to register as wholesale market participants or obtain a generation permit. They access the grid through simplified interconnection rules.

> **⚠ Ceiling updated 2025 — was ≤ 0.5 MW.** The exempt cap is now **< 0.7 MW**: the [[2026-06-09-autoconsumo-cne-2025|CNE DACG de Autoconsumo (12‑XII‑2025)]] *defines* the [[autoconsumo]] permit figure as capacity **≥ 0.7 MW** (Num. 1.7‑I; Art. 30 LSE), so anything below sits in the permit‑free exempt tier. A plant **≥ 0.7 MW leaves Generador Exento status** and must take a [[cne|CNE]] Autoconsumo permit.

## What it enables

A Generador Exento (typically a commercial or industrial user with rooftop solar or small wind):
- Signs an interconnection contract with CFE Distribución to connect to the RGD
- Signs a compensation contract with [[cfe-ssb]] for energy exchange
- Chooses a compensation model ([[medicion-neta]], facturación neta, or venta total)
- Can sell energy to [[cfe-ssb]] or, optionally, to a qualified service supplier (Suministrador de Servicios Calificados) — provided the DG plant has independent metering

## Obligations

- Must comply with technical specs in RES/142/2017 Annex II (bidirectional metering, protection equipment)
- Cannot exceed the capacity established in the interconnection contract
- Must use specified metering equipment (CFE G0000-48 spec)
- Cannot return to prior contract terms once migrated to a new compensation model

## Relationship to GDMTH users

A commercial or industrial user on the [[gdmth]] tariff can simultaneously be a Generador Exento if they install a DG plant **< 0.7 MW**. Their CFE supply contract (GDMTH) and their compensation contract (as Generador Exento) are linked at the same interconnection point under net metering. Scaling generation **to or above 0.7 MW** exits this status into the [[autoconsumo]] permit regime.
